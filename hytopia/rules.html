Hytopia Server Rules

Please read and follow these rules to allow a fair and enjoyable experience for all players.

General Rules

  1. Be respectful to anyone and everyone. If someone is disrespectful toward you (within reason), notify a staff. Do not retaliate.
  2. No harassment, hate speech, or threats of any kind.
  3. Please, try to speak English. You are allowed to play on the server even if you cannot speak it, but all server content, these rules, and chat will be in English.
  4. The following behavior is not welcome and will land you with a ban:
    1. Content or Behavior which could reasonably be considered discriminatory.
    2. Pornographic, distasteful or excessively violent content.
    3. Conent that would generally be illegal to share.
  5. Do not spam.
  6. If you need assistance, refrain from privately messaging a staff and attempt to ask in a public channel.
  7. Avoid mentioning/pinging others without a good reason.

Gameplay Rules

  1. This is NOT 2B2T in any sense. This server leans on the SMP variety, restricting PVP to specific locations, and disallowing any griefing, raiding, or cheating. If caught, a permanant ban will be issued without warning.
  2. Do not be unkind, exploitative or greedy. This includes but is not limited to:
    1. Stealing items.
    2. Aggressively claiming chunks near another player.
    3. Any other behaviors that negatively impact other players' experience.
  3. If you find any bug, exploit, or glitch that allows you to do anything you are not meant to do — within the bounds of common sense — and you precede to take advantage of it, a permanant ban will be issued without warning.
  4. Also, if you come across any bug, exploit, or glitch, please do report it to the people that need to know. Hytale is new, we want to help the game grow. It cannot do that without a community willing to help it grow.

World Rules

  1. As stated previously, this is not 2B2T and thus uncontrolled mass destruction of the terrain near spawn or other's builds and bases will not be tolerated.
  2. Builds must be appropriate. Keep it PG.
  3. Pillars, floating platforms, and unprotected pits will be removed at the request of anyone (within reason). Be tasteful and don't pollute the world.

Extras

  1. Depending on the offense, you will not be given a warning before a permanent ban. We employ a zero-tolerance policy against harmful behavior.
  2. Just because a rule is not on this list, does not mean you can decide Staff is wrong. We have final say. We will be reasonable though.

By playing on this server, you hereby agree to the rules stated above. Failure to follow the rules may result in warnings, mutes, or bans.
